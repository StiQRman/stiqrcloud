Active Cryptographic & Storage Architecture

  • PocketBase-Compatible Schema: Dynamic collections, schema fields, and REST endpoints
  • Reticulum RNS Mesh: Cryptographic destination hashes & multi-hop packet routing
  • Bitcoin Lightning Escrow: Automated proof-of-storage Merkle audits & rent settlement
